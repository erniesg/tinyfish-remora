# IBKR public-share purchase capabilities

Official public sources checked **2026-11-13 (UTC)**. Documentation establishes available interfaces, not this investor’s eligibility or a successful broker connection. No account access, orders, subscriptions, permission changes or activation were performed.

Other OAuth access/approval is **unverified**, not presumed available. |

Fractional quantity and currency-notional are different semantics. A `cashQty` field is not universal stock-notional support. |

This is not a per-venue opening-auction support matrix. | Select a broker-supported mechanism for the exact account/contract/venue. `OPG` or market-on-open must not be assumed usable for every venue or IPO. ## Execution evidence still required

- **Calendar/session:** authoritative venue holidays, half-days, timezone/DST rules, order-entry cutoffs, opening/auction rules and current halt/resumption/tradeability state for each selected listing. No per-venue rules were verified here. Broker server maintenance [11] is not an exchange calendar. A clock trigger only makes a mandate due; it does not prove a session is eligible. Missing evidence blocks rather than silently defers to another session.
- **Account preflight:** correct account and paper/live environment, active brokerage authentication, stock/venue/order permissions, available and settled cash as applicable, currency and fees/FX buffer, data entitlements and fresh executable quotes. None were inspected. No automatic borrowing, funding or FX conversion outside an approved mandate.
- **Bounded authority:** persist account/instrument, trigger/timezone/session/order type, quantity semantics, limit price, aggregate spend including fees/FX buffer, expiry and cancellation policy. Enforce cumulative reservations, revocation and kill/cancel server-side. A bounded mandate may authorise unattended execution within its limits; it does not authorise new access or real-money activation.
- **Recovery:** retain a durable ledger beyond the broker’s recent-order/session window; reconcile broker order identity, cumulative fills, remaining quantity, fees and confirmed cancellation after restart/timeouts. An unresolved submission or cancellation remains reconciliation-required, not safe-to-resubmit or cancelled.

## Validation boundary

The current purchase implementation is **local/mock-only**. Actual-adapter transport tests with injected responses are contract tests, not real IBKR paper or live proof. No authenticated broker, actual paper, IPO or live execution has been verified by this research. Live execution, access, spending, activation and deployment remain held; these unknowns do not block local implementation and deterministic testing.
